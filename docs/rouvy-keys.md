*   **L**: Create a lap

Customizing and Reassigning Keys

You can change or reassign any key binding directly inside the application: 

*   Click your **Profile icon** in the top-left corner of the ROUVY app.

*   Choose **Settings** from the drop-down menu.

*   Go to the **Control** settings tab.

*   Select your keyboard, choose the action you want to modify, and press your preferred new key to confirm. [](https://support.rouvy.com/hc/en-us/articles/47742964491665-Remote-controllers-and-control-mapping)
